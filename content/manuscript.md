# The Human Rendering Pipeline

## MBTI Physics for Graphics Programmers

### The Human Engineering Collection · Volume II

### Preface: the renderer and the scene

Graphics programming is a useful place to study cognition. A renderer turns incomplete descriptions into a visible scene. It allocates a budget, chooses a representation, culls what does not matter, approximates what cannot be computed exactly, and produces feedback in a frame that must arrive on time. Teams do analogous work with attention, interpretation, values, coordination, and trust.

This book calls that analogy **MBTI physics**. It is not physics in the scientific sense, and it is not a claim that cognitive functions are brain modules. It is a compact engineering language for recurring differences in how people sample reality, construct a model, assign value, and move work forward. The word _physics_ points to interaction: forces, fields, constraints, trajectories, and failure modes.

The payoff is practical. You can learn to recognize why an art director asks for a feeling before a specification, why an engine programmer asks for a measurable constraint before a concept, why a technical artist needs a concrete experiment before agreeing to a roadmap, and why an intuitive systems designer may see a future architecture before the rest of the team can name it. None of these is a type verdict. They are perspectives that must cooperate.

### A book of maps

This book studies a familiar human problem: we must take in a changing world, decide what matters, act, learn, and revise. We can do this flexibly, or become captured by one signal, one story, one fear, or one role.

The book brings neuroscience, psychology, personality language, myth, and geometry into conversation. They are not one hidden system. A brain network is not an archetype; a symbolic correspondence is not a physical mechanism. Yet several maps may illuminate the same lived pattern: attention narrows, conflict appears, a missing perspective returns, and a person becomes more able to move.

> Health is not permanent calm or perfect balance. It is recoverable movement: the capacity to enter a state, learn from it, and leave it when the situation changes.

### How claims are marked

| Label      | Meaning                                          | Example                                                |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **KNOWN**  | Established or reasonably well-supported finding | Sleep, threat, pain, and attention affect one another. |
| **MODEL**  | A useful interpretation, not a diagnosis         | A person can become captured by an error signal.       |
| **SYMBOL** | A deliberate metaphor or cultural image          | The center can stand for an integrating self.          |

This distinction is the method. It lets experience remain meaningful without making claims that evidence cannot carry.

# 1. The questions beneath consciousness

Consciousness is not a thing that simply switches on. It is an ongoing coordination problem. A person walking through a crowded street, remembering an argument, deciding whether a sound is dangerous, and planning dinner is continually reorganizing attention, memory, value, action, and feedback.

The process can be expressed as five questions:

```text
What is happening? -> What might it mean? -> What matters? -> What should I do? -> What happened next?
```

This is not a literal diagram of the brain. **MODEL:** it is a practical grammar for experience. Perception supplies material. Prediction proposes an interpretation. Salience and value allocate priority. Action changes the situation. Feedback either confirms or revises the working model.

| Question            | Capacity                | Failure when isolated               |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| What is happening?  | Contact with evidence   | Detail without meaning              |
| What might it mean? | Pattern and possibility | Story mistaken for fact             |
| What matters?       | Value and relationship  | Urgency mistaken for importance     |
| What should I do?   | Agency and execution    | Action without reflection           |
| What happened next? | Learning and correction | Repeating a strategy after it fails |

The important unit is not an individual answer but the circuit. Interpretation needs evidence; action needs values; and all of them need the ability to update.

## Three demands, not three boxes

At any moment, the mind is pulled among exploration, performance, and protection.

```text
                 EXPLORATION
                learn / imagine
                    /\
                   /  \
                  /    \
       PROTECTION -------- PERFORMANCE
        preserve             execute
```

**KNOWN:** distributed brain systems support attention, self-related thought, cognitive control, sensory processing, emotion, and action; no single network is "the consciousness network." Reviews emphasize that attention is dynamic and that attention and salience are related but distinguishable constructs [1, 2]. **MODEL:** exploration asks what else is possible, performance selects and carries out a course, and protection detects cost or danger. Each is necessary. Trouble begins when one demands permanent rule.

Exploration without selection disperses. Performance without exploration becomes brittle. Protection without recovery turns the whole world into a warning.

# 2. Attention, salience, and prediction

Attention is an allocation system. It decides what receives processing, what is ignored, and what returns again and again. Salience is the felt or learned importance attached to a signal: a baby's cry, a name across a room, a bodily sensation, a notification, an unfinished task.

Prediction makes attention efficient. The nervous system does not wait passively for a complete picture; it compares incoming signals with expectations and devotes resources to what seems important, surprising, rewarding, or threatening. **KNOWN:** prediction, learning, sensory filtering, and attention are intertwined across many systems; salience models and predictive accounts offer related but not identical explanations [3, 4]. **MODEL:** suffering often increases when the system gives a single kind of signal too much priority.

```text
working model -> prediction -> input -> mismatch -> update
                         ^                 |
                         +---- attention --+
```

Prediction is not the enemy. Without it, every moment would be unintelligible. It becomes a problem when the update step is blocked: when an alarm cannot be silenced, when a memory is treated as current danger, or when a compelling pattern is never tested against alternatives.

## A checking practice

When a signal becomes intense, ask:

1. What can I observe directly?
2. What am I inferring?
3. What else could explain this?
4. What action is reversible and proportionate?
5. What feedback would change my mind?

This is not cold skepticism. It protects meaning from overreach.

# 3. Openness and constraint

The book's central polarity is not reason versus emotion, or science versus spirituality. It is openness and constraint.

Openness allows novelty, association, learning, play, imagination, and revision. Constraint provides boundary, selection, memory, inhibition, responsibility, and form. A healthy system needs both.

| More openness can offer           | When unbounded                         | More constraint can offer       | When unbounded                    |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Creativity, curiosity, connection | Overload, distractibility, grandiosity | Reliability, safety, completion | Rigidity, compulsion, overcontrol |

> Generation without evaluation becomes chaos. Evaluation without generation becomes sterility.

Creative work benefits from a rhythm:

```text
collect -> associate -> select -> make -> test -> revise -> rest -> return
```

Rest matters. A mind cannot remain in search mode forever. Completion is not the death of possibility; it is what allows feedback to enter.

# 4. When useful loops become traps

Many difficult states can be described, cautiously, as a useful protection or inference process becoming hard to exit. This is not a replacement for diagnosis, treatment, or individual history. It is a shared lens.

| Experience                | Useful function                         | Capture pattern                                       | Restoring movement                                            |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| OCD-like loops [17]       | Detect error and uncertainty            | Checking briefly reduces distress, reinforcing return | Tolerate uncertainty with appropriate clinical support        |
| Anxiety                   | Prepare for threat                      | Forecast is treated as present fact                   | Ground in present evidence and proportionate action           |
| ADHD experiences [15]     | Rapid salience shifting and association | Attention follows novelty, urgency, or reward         | External structure, transition support, meaningful cues       |
| Autistic experiences [16] | Deep focus, precision, stable interests | Switching and high sensory load can be costly         | Predictable transitions, sensory accommodation, recovery time |
| Depressive rumination     | Learn from loss and error               | Negative model repeats without new information        | Connection, action at a usable scale, professional care       |
| Elevated mood states [21] | Energy, confidence, association         | Significance and activation outrun checking           | Sleep protection, slower decisions, clinical support          |

The table does not classify people. It identifies questions: What signal has become dominant? What corrective information has become unavailable? What would make a small switch possible?

## The error signal that will not close

An ordinary concern should move through a cycle: notice, assess, respond, and release. In obsessive-compulsive patterns, the relief after checking or neutralizing can teach the system to return to the same doubt.

```text
uncertainty -> alarm -> compulsion -> brief relief -> stronger future alarm
```

The point is not to argue a person out of a thought. It is to change the learned relationship to uncertainty, often with evidence-based professional treatment.

# 5. Time, pain, and the protective mind

Pain, trauma, and migraine illustrate a profound fact: the experienced world is shaped by state, context, expectation, attention, and the body. This does not mean these experiences are imagined. It means the nervous system is an active protective system, not a camera or a damage meter.

**KNOWN:** migraine aura is strongly associated with cortical spreading depolarization, and migraine can include dramatic changes in sensitivity to light, sound, smell, or vision [20, 26]. **KNOWN:** persistent pain is influenced by biological, psychological, and social factors as well as tissue state [19]. **KNOWN:** trauma can alter threat learning, arousal, memory, and present-moment safety [18]. These statements do not reduce any condition to a thought pattern.

**MODEL:** a traumatic memory can be experienced as insufficiently placed in the past. A useful recovery sentence is: _That happened. It matters. It is not happening now._ It is not a cure. It is an orientation practice that supports the work of restoring time, body, context, and safety.

```text
pain -> fear -> attention -> tension / avoidance -> more pain
```

This loop is not universal and should never be used to dismiss pain. It names one pathway through which protection can amplify itself. Medical assessment is essential for new, severe, changing, or unexplained symptoms.

## Safety before interpretation

When reality testing, sleep, functioning, safety, or self-care is substantially impaired, a symbolic interpretation is not the first task. Contact qualified medical or mental-health support, reduce immediate risk, involve trusted people, and postpone irreversible decisions. No personality model explains away coercion, psychosis, mania, self-harm risk, severe substance effects, or medical symptoms.

# 6. Eight ways of attending

Jungian and MBTI-derived function language is useful as a vocabulary of attention, not as neuroscience and not as a personality verdict. Personality measures are self-report models with reliability, validity, interpretation, and cultural limits; they should not be treated as transparent access to a person [5, 6]. Here the functions ask what a mind tends to notice or trust.

| Perspective | Question                     | Contribution                         | Blind spot when overused               |
| ----------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Se          | What is here now?            | Direct evidence and responsiveness   | Present detail without direction       |
| Si          | What does this resemble?     | Continuity and learned experience    | Precedent treated as destiny           |
| Ne          | What else could this become? | Possibility and association          | Connection treated as proof            |
| Ni          | Where is this leading?       | Pattern and trajectory               | Interpretation insulated from evidence |
| Ti          | Does this make sense?        | Internal precision                   | Model completion over human context    |
| Te          | What works externally?       | Implementation and measurement       | Efficiency without values              |
| Fi          | What matters to me?          | Personal conscience and authenticity | Conviction without correction          |
| Fe          | What matters between us?     | Relationship and shared context      | Consensus treated as truth             |

The opposites are useful error-correctors:

```text
Se <-> Ni     evidence <-> interpretation
Si <-> Ne     precedent <-> possibility
Ti <-> Fe     logical clarity <-> human context
Te <-> Fi     effectiveness <-> values
```

No skill belongs to one perspective. Strategy may require foresight, evidence, execution, values, and feedback. Empathy may require resonance, boundaries, curiosity, and accuracy. **MODEL:** functions are instruments; skills are ensembles; meta-awareness is the conductor.

# 7. Shadow, power, and reality testing

Every cognitive strength has a shadow: not an evil essence, but a capacity to become self-sealing or to be used without accountability. Imagination can manufacture certainty. Precision can rationalize cruelty. Social insight can become pressure. Efficiency can turn people into inputs.

The practical question is never "Which type is dangerous?" It is: what happens when competence separates from consent, impact, accountability, and repair?

| Capacity            | Constructive use        | Shadow use                            | Corrective                        |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Pattern recognition | Anticipate consequences | Claim hidden certainty                | Ask for disconfirming evidence    |
| Social attunement   | Care and coordination   | Shame, exclusion, dependency          | Protect consent and independence  |
| Analysis            | Clarify mechanisms      | Rationalize or endlessly defer action | Test against effects on people    |
| Execution           | Build reliable systems  | Control and coercion                  | State values and permit challenge |

Manipulation can be understood as adversarial interference with another person's reality testing: narrowing alternatives, distorting feedback, inducing urgency, or making belonging conditional. This is a social pattern, not a personality type. Its counterweights are time, outside perspectives, clear records, boundaries, and permission to question.

## The witness

```text
"I am afraid"             -> fear is occurring
"This is certainly true"  -> my mind has produced a compelling model
"I must act now"          -> urgency is present; what is proportionate?
```

The witness is not a detached, superior self. It is the capacity to observe an experience without allowing it to become the whole world.

# 8. Altered states without a ladder

Dreams, meditation, flow, trance, intense music, breath practices, sleep loss, sensory deprivation, substances, and acute stress can change attention, time, self-boundary, association, and sensory processing. Such states can be beautiful, frightening, creative, or destabilizing. They are not a ladder on which more unusual automatically means more true.

| Dimension         | Question                                                  |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sensory intensity | Is more signal entering awareness?                        |
| Association       | Are more connections being generated?                     |
| Self-boundary     | Does the ordinary sense of self feel more or less stable? |
| Executive control | Can the person pause, choose, and revise?                 |
| Integration       | Can the experience be put into context afterward?         |

> More connection is not necessarily more truth.

Music offers a gentle example. A melody requires memory of a previous note, perception of the present note, and anticipation of the next. Rhythm, breath, posture, ritual, and coordinated movement can regulate attention, arousal, timing, and social connection. None of this requires a claim that music reveals hidden cosmic facts.

When an altered state includes severe insomnia, paranoia, loss of reality testing, dangerous impulsivity, or inability to function, the priority is safety and clinical support, not interpretation [21, 22].

# 9. Center, boundary, and relationship

Geometry is useful because it makes relationships visible. A point can represent a center; a circle a boundary; two circles an encounter; their intersection a space held by both without being owned by either.

```text
point -> circle -> two circles -> intersection -> wider pattern
```

**KNOWN:** six equal circles can surround one equal circle in a plane, forming sixfold symmetry. **SYMBOL:** repeated relationship can create complexity around a center. The symbolic reading is not a discovery hidden inside geometry; it is an invitation to think.

The vesica piscis, the almond-shaped overlap between two circles, can symbolize a relational third: not you, not me, but the space of contact. In psychological language, it resembles the capacity to hold a conflict long enough for a new response to emerge. In Jungian language, this can be compared, carefully, with the transcendent function: a mediating result that emerges from sustained relation between conscious and unconscious positions [25]. In Christian imagery, the cross can be read as a meeting of vertical and horizontal dimensions: heaven and earth, self and other. These are interpretations, not reductions of history or faith.

The center is not the absence of fear, ego, desire, or thought. It is an organizing capacity that lets different states appear without any one becoming sovereign.

# 10. Numbers, planets, and symbolic discipline

Numbers are excellent teachers when their mathematical character is clear and poor guides when symbolism is presented as proof.

| Pattern | Fact                                                     | Possible symbolic use                        | Boundary                                  |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 3       | Three non-collinear points establish a plane             | Relationship can produce a third possibility | Not proof that all triads share one cause |
| 4       | Four is common in directions, seasons, and schemas       | Grounding and orientation                    | Traditions use different quaternities     |
| 6       | Hexagonal symmetry and circle packing are real           | Multiplicity around a center                 | No energy claim follows                   |
| 9       | Digital-root patterns have regular arithmetic properties | Completion or return                         | Not evidence of hidden physics            |
| 12      | Calendars and cultures often use twelve                  | Cyclical order                               | No universal privileged number            |

Planetary language can likewise name psychological weather. Sun may symbolize integration; Mars action; Jupiter expansion; Saturn boundary; Mercury analysis; Venus value; Neptune imagination; Pluto transformation. This is a constructed archetypal map, not an account of causation.

**KNOWN:** Earth has Schumann resonances in the electromagnetic cavity between its surface and lower ionosphere [27]. Light received by the eyes is a major timing cue for the suprachiasmatic nucleus and the wider circadian system [28]. **NOT ESTABLISHED:** that a planetary frequency activates a particular cognitive function, or that a person becomes neurologically synchronized with Earth at a special frequency. The subjective feeling of synchrony can be genuine without proving an external transmission.

# 11. The social field and the ecology of attention

We do not regulate attention alone. Families, institutions, groups, screens, incentives, and stories shape what becomes visible and what becomes costly to say. The social field can support integration through honest feedback and belonging without conformity. It can also create capture through outrage, surveillance, scarcity, or identity pressure.

Think of attention as an ecology. What is rewarded grows. What is punished disappears. What is repeated becomes familiar, and familiarity can be mistaken for truth.

## Four protections for a shared reality

1. **Plural feedback.** Important beliefs should encounter people and evidence outside one closed group.
2. **Time.** Urgency is a powerful influence tactic; delay protects choice when the decision is reversible.
3. **Traceability.** Records, sources, and clear commitments help memory resist distortion.
4. **Repair.** Healthy systems can acknowledge error without treating revision as betrayal.

A good learning environment alternates opening and closing: generate ideas, inspect evidence, make something, receive feedback, and revise. A good leader does not merely create momentum; they make dissent and correction possible.

# 12. The geometry of return

The book began with five questions and three demands. It ends with a practice of return.

```text
signal -> attention -> meaning -> action -> habit -> identity
                                  |                    |
                                  +-- feedback <- awareness <- conflict
```

Return does not mean going backward or becoming unchanged. It means recovering contact with what a dominant state hid: evidence when imagination runs ahead, possibility when precedent hardens, values when efficiency takes over, relationship when analysis isolates, rest when action will not stop.

## A compact integration practice

When you notice capture:

1. Name the state without turning it into an identity.
2. Locate the dominant signal: threat, urgency, certainty, novelty, pain, approval, or control.
3. Ask which perspective has become invisible.
4. Make one small, reversible contact with that missing perspective.
5. Protect sleep, body, relationship, and safety before making large claims or irreversible decisions.
6. Review what changed, and revise the model.

This is not a promise of mastery. It is a repeatable way to increase freedom. The center is not a fixed state; it is the ability to move among states without being trapped by any one of them.

## Afterword: what this book does and does not claim

This book does not claim that a type explains a person, that symptoms are symbolic messages to decode, that ancient traditions secretly anticipated neuroscience, or that geometry and planets cause mental states. It claims something more useful: human beings repeatedly face the work of attention, interpretation, value, action, correction, and integration. Different maps can help if we keep their boundaries visible.

The standard is not loyalty to a map. It is better contact with evidence, possibility, consequence, value, and one another. A useful model makes revision easier, returns agency to the people involved, and leaves reality larger than the explanation. When it stops doing that, return to observation and begin again.
